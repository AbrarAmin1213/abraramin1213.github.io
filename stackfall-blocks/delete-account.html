Delete your account and data

Applies to the Stackfall Blocks game on Android and iOS.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Stackfall Blocks.
  2. Tap the gear icon on the home screen to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete everything.

You need an internet connection. Everything listed below is deleted immediately, and the game starts over as a new player.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the game)

Email abraramin1@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Stackfall Blocks account”. Include:

  • the email address you saved your progress with, if you did; or
  • your player name and roughly your best score, so we can find your leaderboard entries.

We'll delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address if you saved your progress with one.
  • Your cloud profile: player name, coins, scores, Adventure progress, Daily puzzle history, themes, purchase status and settings.
  • All of your leaderboard entries (weekly, all-time and lines cleared).
  • When deleting in the game, the progress saved on your phone as well.

What is kept

We keep nothing on our servers after deletion, including in backups. Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and advertising data by Google AdMob, under their own policies. Deleting your account does not refund purchases; if you reinstall, Restore purchases in the shop can bring back Remove ads using your store account.

To only clear some data (for example your leaderboard entries) without deleting your account, email us at the same address.

See also the Stackfall Blocks privacy policy.
